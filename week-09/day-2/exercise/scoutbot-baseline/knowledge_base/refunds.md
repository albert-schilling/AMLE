# Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of the original purchase date. Refund requests made
after 30 days are handled at the discretion of the support team and usually require a manager's
approval.

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days. Refunds to a bank
transfer or a prepaid card can take up to 10 business days, because the receiving bank controls the
final step and we cannot speed it up from our side.

To request a refund, the customer must provide their order number and a short reason for the return.
Without an order number we cannot locate the purchase, so that is the first thing to ask for if it is
missing.

Digital goods, including downloadable files and activation keys, are non-refundable once they have
been downloaded or the key has been revealed. This is stated at checkout and is the most common
source of refund disputes.

Subscription plans are refunded on a prorated basis from the date of cancellation, not the date of
the original charge. A customer who cancels halfway through a billing month is refunded for the
remaining half.

Shipping fees are not refundable unless the return is the result of our error, such as a wrong or
damaged item. In those cases the original shipping fee is refunded in full alongside the item cost.
