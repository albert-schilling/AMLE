# Shipping and Delivery

Standard shipping is free on all orders over 50 euros. Orders below that threshold pay a flat 4.90
euro shipping fee, calculated at checkout before payment.

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days within the EU. Express delivery, available for an extra
9.90 euros, arrives within 1 to 2 business days for orders placed before 2pm on a working day.

We ship to all EU member states. We do not currently ship outside the EU, and an order with a
non-EU delivery address will be cancelled and refunded automatically.

Once an order ships, the customer receives a tracking link by email. The link becomes active within
a few hours of dispatch, so a link that shows no data immediately after an order is normal and not a
sign of a lost parcel.

If a parcel is marked delivered but the customer cannot find it, ask them to check with neighbours
and the building's reception first, then wait 24 hours, since carriers sometimes scan a parcel as
delivered a day before it arrives. If it is still missing after that, open a lost-parcel claim with
the order number.

Undelivered parcels are returned to our warehouse after three failed delivery attempts. A returned
parcel is refunded minus the original shipping fee, unless the failure was our error.
