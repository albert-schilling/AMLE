# Billing and Subscriptions

Subscriptions renew automatically on the same day of the month as the original sign-up. The customer
is charged to the payment method on file and receives an invoice by email on the renewal date.

A customer can cancel a subscription at any time from their account settings. Cancellation stops the
next renewal; it does not refund the current period, though the plan stays active until the end of
the period already paid for.

If a renewal payment fails, we retry it three times over six days. The subscription is paused after
the third failure, and the customer keeps read-only access until the payment succeeds. We never
delete data because of a failed payment.

Upgrades take effect immediately and are charged as a prorated amount for the rest of the current
period. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal, so the customer keeps the higher plan until the
period they already paid for runs out.

Duplicate charges are almost always a temporary authorisation hold rather than a real second charge.
An authorisation hold disappears on its own within a few business days. If two settled charges appear
for the same order, that is a genuine double charge and is refunded in full.

VAT is included in all listed prices for EU customers. A business customer with a valid VAT number
can enter it in account settings to receive a VAT-exclusive invoice.
