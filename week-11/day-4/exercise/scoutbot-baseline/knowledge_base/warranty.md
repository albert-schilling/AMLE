# Warranty and Repairs

All hardware products come with a two-year warranty that covers manufacturing defects. The warranty
starts on the delivery date, not the order date, so a delayed shipment does not shorten the coverage.

The warranty covers faults in materials and workmanship: a button that stops responding, a battery
that will not hold a charge, a seam that comes apart under normal use. It does not cover accidental
damage, liquid damage, or normal wear such as scratches and fading.

To start a warranty claim, the customer sends a photo or short video of the fault along with the
order number. Most claims are resolved by shipping a replacement part; the customer does not usually
need to return the whole product first.

If a replacement part cannot fix the fault, we send a full replacement unit. If the same product has
failed twice under warranty, the customer may choose a refund instead of a third replacement.

Repairs carried out by a third party, or any attempt to open a sealed unit, void the warranty. This
is worth checking early in a claim, because a customer who has already opened the device is no longer
covered and the conversation changes.

Out-of-warranty repairs are offered at a flat fee that depends on the product category. The support
team can quote the fee but cannot waive it without a manager's approval.
